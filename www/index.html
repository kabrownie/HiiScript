Kabrownie Screen - quick guide

Plain text on the left, formatted screenplay on the right. Everything is saved on this device automatically.

Formatting

INT. HOUSE - DAY creates a scene heading. Uppercase lines become characters, following lines become dialogue, and (whispering) becomes a parenthetical. Use ! for action, > for transitions, # for sections, = for synopsis, and === for page breaks.

Keyboard

Tab cycles line types. Ctrl/⌘+S saves. Ctrl/⌘+1 through 6 set line types. Export Fountain regularly for a portable plain-text backup.
