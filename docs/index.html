Write the scene, keep the file.

Hiiscript is a free, open-source screenplay writer that works offline. Write in Fountain, see screenplay formatting as you type, and keep your drafts on your own device.

Download latest releaseView on GitHub

Offline first

Your scripts, settings, characters, and scenes stay on your device. No account is required.

Built for screenplays

Use title pages, dialogue formatting, page breaks, autocomplete, preview, PDF printing, and Fountain export.

Open source

Inspect, modify, and self-host the project from its public GitHub repository.

Privacy

Hiiscript has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or background screenplay uploads. PDF import may download PDF.js from a public CDN the first time it is used.

Read the full details in the privacy section of the README.
